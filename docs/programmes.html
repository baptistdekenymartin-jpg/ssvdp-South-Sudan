HOW WE SERVE

How We Serve

At SSVP South Sudan, we accompany vulnerable individuals, families and communities through practical acts of charity, compassion and sustainable development. Our work responds to immediate humanitarian needs while creating long-term opportunities for self-reliance, dignity and hope. Through healthcare, education, vocational skills training, emergency relief, food security, agricultural initiatives, child and family support, and community development programmes, we strive to improve lives and strengthen communities across the areas where we serve.

PROGRAMMES

Our Programme Areas

Our work combines humanitarian response, social care, education, health and livelihood development to help vulnerable communities build safer and more self-reliant lives.

Vocational Training

Practical skills development for young people and women through tailoring, building and construction, welding, general electricity, automobile mechanics, computer literacy and other trades.

Learn More

Education

Basic and primary education, school support and learning opportunities for vulnerable children.

Learn More

Healthcare Services

Primary healthcare, maternal services, medical support and community health services for people who cannot afford essential care.

Learn More

Child Care and Protection

Support for street-connected children, child welfare, safe care, education and long-term development through programmes such as Be in Hope Home.

Learn More

Food Security and Nutrition

Supplementary feeding, nutrition support, school snacks and food assistance for malnourished children and vulnerable families.

Learn More

Agriculture and Livelihoods

Agricultural training, women's agribusiness, sustainable farming and livelihood support for IDPs and vulnerable communities.

Learn More

Humanitarian Assistance

Emergency relief, food distributions, plastic-sheet support, cash assistance and services for internally displaced people and refugees.

Learn More

Social Enterprise and Community Development

Income-generating initiatives such as the Lologo jam-production activity, women's empowerment, community capacity building and self-reliance programmes.
